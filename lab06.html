Hometown Map

Reflection

Design Inspiration

For my basemap design, I wanted something that felt simple but still visually interesting. I was inspired by modern maps that use softer and more minimal colors. When I was looking through different photos and inspiration, I knew I wanted a muted base with small pops of color for the different features. In Mapbox, I selected colors based on a photo I took in San Diego. I used the colors from the rocks as inspiration for the base of the map, while incorporating the greens and pinks from the image into features like the roads and labels. I wanted the map to feel calm and natural while still making the important elements easy to see.

Cartographic Challenges

One of the main challenges I faced was thinking about how these colors would contrast on a map. Originally, I used a darker base and quickly realized it made the rest of the map much harder to see. Because the labels and roads are important for navigation, I wanted the base to stay more neutral so those elements could stand out. Another issue was that the green roads originally made the map look cluttered. After experimenting with the settings, I adjusted the width of the roads so they were thinner, which made the map much easier to look at and understand.

Technical Challenges

One of the technical challenges I encountered was integrating the custom Mapbox style with Leaflet. Ensuring that the map tiles loaded correctly and that the interactive elements like markers and popups functioned smoothly required careful attention to the API documentation and some trial and error. Additionally, optimizing the map for performance while maintaining visual quality was a key consideration throughout the development process.The technical part of this assignment was where I struggled the most. I am not very confident in Python, so I used ChatGPT to help me write the base structure of the code. However, I asked it to leave some parts blank so that I could go in and add my own information, such as my Mapbox token, style ID, and file names. This helped me better understand where those pieces needed to go in the script. After that, I tested the code and made sure it connected correctly with my CSV file and Mapbox style. Looking at the final result, I honestly do not think this assignment would have been possible for me without the help of AI, but it did help me understand the process much better.
